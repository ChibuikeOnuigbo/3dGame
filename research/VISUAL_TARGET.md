# VISUAL TARGET — Pump Station 7

This is the **visual contract** every room, asset, material and light must satisfy.
Anything that does not match this document is a rejection candidate regardless of
how "detailed" it is.

## Concept
An **abandoned coastal water-pumping station** — not a bunker, not a spaceship, not
a haunted house. A place where real people did a real job (move water), and then
left in a hurry. The horror comes from *absence, wrongness and machinery*, not from
skulls-on-walls.

## Primary references (from the supplied videos)
| Reference | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| INFRA | Real, functional, aged water/utility infrastructure; walk-and-inspect pacing |
| SOMA | Underwater/coastal facility dread; pressure-vessel and pump machinery |
| Amnesia: The Bunker | One connected facility; generator/power as a player goal |
| Alien: Isolation | Industrial corridors, sightlines, machinery noise, persistent threat |
| Black Mesa / Portal 2 | Facility architecture that guides without UI; environmental storytelling |

## Architecture
- **Ceiling height:** 3.0–4.5 m in rooms; 3.0 m in corridors. High enough to feel
  industrial, low enough to feel confined.
- **Corridors:** 1.6–2.4 m wide. Main spine wider (2.4 m) with overhead pipe runs;
  side corridors narrower (1.6 m) for tension.
- **Doors:** steel industrial doors, 2.0 m high × 0.9 m wide; heavy frames, lever
  handles, some with a small wired-glass vision panel. Painted, scratched.
- **Walls:** cast-in-place concrete with form-tie marks and control joints; lower
  sections tiled in wet rooms; paint band up to ~1.5 m in corridors.
- **Floors:** sealed concrete with worn walk-lines, deck grating in plant rooms,
  tiles in washrooms, grates over floor drains.
- **Pipe runs:** exposed painted steel pipe with flanges, valves, and hangers along
  ceilings and walls. Pipes must connect between plausible endpoints — never dead-end
  mid-air.
- **Electrical:** steel conduit along walls, junction boxes, fluorescent battens with
  steel wire guards, emergency luminaires, wall-mounted disconnects.

## Materials (PBR, one language)
- **Concrete:** rough, uneven tone, staining, chips, seams, water marks, tie holes.
- **Metal:** roughness variation, scratches, oxidation, dirt in recesses. Painted
  steel for pipes/handrails; raw/weathered steel for machinery.
- **Paint:** faded, chipped, layered, dirty (the classic institutional green/cream
  over grey primer).
- **Glass:** restrained reflections; wired glass where safety glass belongs.
- **Plastic:** believable roughness, subtle aging, no glossy toy finish.
- **Wood:** only where it belongs (office desks, crates, shelving) — real grain, wear.

## Color palette
- Base: concrete greys, aged cream/off-white, faded institutional green.
- Accent: safety yellow (only on hazards/handrails), hazard red (only on valves,
  stops, emergency gear), cyan/green phosphor of fluorescent light.
- **No** rainbow/neon overload, no saturated blue/red scene fills, no unmotivated
  purple "horror" wash.

## Lighting (motivated only)
- Fluorescent battens (cool white, flicker where wrong), utility lamps, emergency
  lights, machine task lights, exterior daylight/moonlight, window light.
- Darkness is *earned*: some rooms are normal-bright so abnormal rooms land.
- One or two broken/flickering fixtures max per area — never a disco.

## Weathering & dirt
- Water stains under leaks, salt corrosion near the sea gate, dust in disused rooms,
  boot-wear on floors, hand-wear on handles. Age is visible but not cartoonish.

## Prop density
- **Hero objects** (2–5 per room): the thing the room is *for* (pump, generator,
  switchgear, desk, cot).
- **Functional support** (5–15): what the workers actually needed there.
- **Micro-dressing:** only where it tells story. Negative space is intentional.
- One coherent object group (a half-finished maintenance job) beats 20 random props.

## Scale & camera
- Real-world metric scale. Camera height **1.6 m**. FOV ~75°.
- Doors/pipes/furniture must read as real size — no oversized toy props, no tiny
  fixtures.

## Horror style
- Anticipation, absence, sound, distant movement, things almost-but-not-quite normal.
- **No** random jump scares, no spam of events. Restraint above all.

## UI / guidance
- Minimal HUD: objective line + interaction prompt only.
- Guidance via light, composition, signage, open/locked doors, landmarks.
