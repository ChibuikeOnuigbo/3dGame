# HOLLOW CURRENT — Story bible

Working title remains **Hollow Current**. Setting: **Pump Station 7**, a coastal municipal water facility, not a maze brand.

## 1. Premise

**Who.** You are a night contractor — electrician / pump tech — called in after the day shift ghosted a ticket: *Bus A dead. Fuse tray empty. Tide alarm looping.*

**Where.** Station 7 sits on a rock bowl above a sea intake. Reception, security, offices, workshop, generator hall, Lab B (CURRENT-7 silt program), maintenance crawl, valve gallery, yard, shack, sea-gate.

**Why you are there.** Overtime. Badge already printed. Someone typed your name into reception before you arrived.

**Before.** The station sampled “hollow current” — pressure pulses in the intake that do not match tide tables. Jars in Lab B. A keypad code in archives. Day crew left mid-shift.

**Now.** Lockdown notice on the glass. Generator cold. Lab B reader waiting. Valves misaligned. Something walks the generator catwalk and the rain-yard.

**Goal (believed).** Restore power, align valves, open the sea-gate, go home.

**Goal (actual).** The station is using you as a live fuse: your movement through the pipes “completes” a circuit the silt samples were mapping. The sea-gate is not an exit until the current has a body.

**Revelation.** The empty CURRENT-7 jar was not stolen. It was poured. The delayed knock you hear in the pipes is your own footsteps, out of phase. Leaving through the gate does not end the loop; it exports it.

Keep exposition on notices, terminals, and objects. No cutscene dump.

## 2. Chapters

### Ch.0 — Arrival / Reception
- **Narrative:** You are expected. The building is not.
- **Gameplay:** Walk, look, interact, flashlight, first door.
- **Environment:** Civic paint, tile, glass notice, two desks still “on duty.”
- **Horror:** Quiet that is too organized.
- **Escalation:** Baseline.
- **Reward:** Lockdown list (fuse → generator → card → Lab B → valves → archive code → sea-gate).
- **Foreshadow:** Name already on the terminal.

### Ch.1 — Security & card
- **Narrative:** Authority without people.
- **Gameplay:** Take Lab B card; learn locked readers.
- **Environment:** Console, empty chair, scuffed floor arc.
- **Horror:** Someone watched cameras until they didn’t.
- **Reward:** Card.
- **Foreshadow:** Scorch later in generator matches the floor scar here.

### Ch.2 — Spine corridor / offices / storage / workshop / crew wing
- **Narrative:** Work interrupted, not wrecked. The crew wing (canteen, bunks, locker room) still holds the day shift's unfinished coffee, unmade cots, and a roster with your name on it.
- **Gameplay:** Fuse in workshop; office terminal as compass; storage as optional loot; crew wing as optional discovery (canteen coffee urn, cots, shift roster, wall phone).
- **Environment:** Wainscot, pipes, labeled shelves, tool chest; kitchen run, bunks, showers.
- **Horror:** Coffee still warm-looking. Tools laid out for a job you are now doing. One cot stripped; its locker already empty.
- **Reward:** 60A fuse + optional lore.
- **Foreshadow:** “Don’t stay after the pumps change pitch.”

### Ch.3 — Generator hall + boiler room
- **Narrative:** The heart is cold until you feed it; the boiler room below it keeps its own record of the pressure.
- **Gameplay:** Install fuse, start bus; first drone patrol; catwalk. Optional: pressure log in the boiler room.
- **Environment:** Scale, amber light, tank, stairs; hoppers, pipe runs, pistons.
- **Horror:** After start, pipes knock once — then wait. The boiler log shows spikes that match no tide table.
- **Reward:** Power / Lab path.
- **Foreshadow:** Arc on the floor: something heavy swung more than once. The knock is already in the pressure record.

### Ch.4 — Lab B
- **Narrative:** CURRENT-7 is not municipal.
- **Gameplay:** Card reader; inspect jars.
- **Environment:** Tile, benches, empty jar, lid inside-out.
- **Horror:** Science that stopped mid-protocol.
- **Reward:** Maintenance shutter.
- **Foreshadow:** Silt “closer every year” (archives later).

### Ch.5 — Maintenance squeeze
- **Narrative:** The building tries to keep you.
- **Gameplay:** Timed traverse; walls close if you linger center.
- **Environment:** Narrow, pipes, no door leaves (intentional pinch).
- **Horror:** Architecture as predator.
- **Reward:** Valve gallery.
- **Foreshadow:** Same knock as generator, closer.

### Ch.6 — Valves + tunnel
- **Narrative:** You finish the day shift’s last job.
- **Gameplay:** Three valves; 3/3 unlocks pump interlock.
- **Environment:** Wheels, gauges, barrel, wet floor.
- **Horror:** Each valve changes the ambient pitch.
- **Reward:** Yard access.
- **Foreshadow:** Tide noise on the shack radio.

### Ch.7 — Yard, shack, sea-gate
- **Narrative:** Outside is not safety.
- **Gameplay:** Rain, drones, radio, keypad 4-7-2-1 from archives (loop back).
- **Environment:** Asphalt, fence, shack wood, gate motors.
- **Horror:** Open space, nowhere to hide well.
- **Reward:** Ending beat at the gate.
- **Foreshadow:** Delayed knock = your gait.

### Ch.8 — Archives (side wing, required for code)
- **Narrative:** Paper remembers what people won’t.
- **Gameplay:** Drawer → code; optional lore.
- **Environment:** Wood, cabinets, photos of tide lines.
- **Horror:** Photos dated next year.
- **Reward:** 4-7-2-1.
- **Foreshadow:** “Closer every year.”

## 3. Core loop (in this building)

Explore → notice wrong (name, empty jar, pitch) → interact → survive (drone / squeeze) → short problem (fuse, valves, code) → new area → information → repeat.

## 4. Room existence test

If a space can be deleted without losing a beat of the loop above, it does not ship. Reception, security, corridor, offices, archives, storage, workshop, generator, lab, maintenance, valves, tunnel, yard, shack, sea-gate each have a unique job — plus the crew wing (east hall, canteen, bunks, locker), the boiler room, and deep stores (store B), each added to earn its place rather than to pad the map.

## 5. Style ranks (skill, not kills)

NORMAL → CLEAN → INSANE → MASTER  
Earned by momentum, no-damage stretches, optional secrets, fast encounters, risky routes.

## 6. Threats (few, readable)

- **Stalker-drone:** patrol, chase on noise, silhouette + amber lamp.
- **Squeeze:** maintenance walls.
- **Watcher (later):** camera that changes room state if stared at.

## 7. Tone rules

Not Backrooms. Not walking sim. Not jumpscare chain. Industrial civic decay, 70/20/10. Empty only when exposure is the point (yard, squeeze).
